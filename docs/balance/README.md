## The runner (Phase 7)

`npm run balance -- [options]` (`tools/balance/run.ts`) plays **recipes × seeds × death rules × presets × scripted players** through `tools/balance/collect.ts`, and writes `docs/balance/<date>-<tag>.md` (this folder) and `tools/balance/out/<date>-<tag>.json` (git-ignored; every delve and every floor as rows). A full Gravehollow pass takes several minutes, so start it in the background.

**What it plays is data.** `content/tuning.json` → `runner` holds the recipes (a party's classes, level, gear grade, pet stance, zone and clear target), the targets, the seed count, the command cap and the careful player's knobs; lint rule 63 checks them. The Cellars' eight recipes are D-084's with James's targets; the Crypts' and the Vault's mirror them at each zone's recommended levels (`07` §1.2) — Proposed until checkpoint 2. A recipe may play part of its zone (`floors`): the Drowned Vault is judged as two delves, F7 (Sister Naesa) and a start on F8 (the Hollow King) at the level the F7 party reaches there (D-083: "the next delve may start on the floor below"); the `vault-run` set plays F7 to F8 in one delve and is reported, not judged. R comes from the sim (`economy.ts` `rForFloor` at the delve's first floor).

| Option | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `--seeds N` · `--seed-start S` | `runner.seeds` · 1 | seeds per cell |
| `--tag T` · `--date D` | `gravehollow` · today | the report's name |
| `--zones 1,2,3` | every zone the content has | recipes of these zones (a delve is one zone, D-098) |
| `--floors A-B` · `--whole` | each recipe's zone | every recipe from floor A to floor B, across zones; `--whole` is F1 to the last floor |
| `--sets main,dive` | `main` | `dive` holds the threat check: a party that goes too far (D-092) |
| `--recipes id,…` · `--sizes 1,2,3,4` | all | pick recipes by id or party size |
| `--death sheltered,mortal,nightmare` · `--death all` | `mortal` | the death rules (D-088, D-095) |
| `--difficulty normal,hard,dread` · `--pacts a,b` | `normal` · none | presets and Pacts through the game's own `DelveOptions` |
| `--policy textbook,careful,…` | `runner.policy` | the scripted players (`sim/src/policies.ts`) |
| `--cap N` · `--no-class-damage` · `--fixture` | `runner.commandCap` | the command cap; skip pass A's class-damage measure; play the Phase 2 fixture content |
| `--plan gravehollow` | none | a named plan from `runner.plans`: several sub-matrices in one report, each at its own seed count (the Phase 7 pass: the main matrix, the careful player, Hard and Dread, the dive set, the one-delve Vault run) |
| `--kills first\|repeat` | `runner.bossKills` (`repeat`) | whether the delves' bosses have died before (08 §11's repeat rows, the steady state R measures) or pay their first-kill lines |
| `--set kind:path=value,…` | none | a what-if: changes the loaded content in memory only (`monster:hollow-king.multipliers.hp=22`, `tuning:dungeon.partyScaling.hp.1=0.85`, `loot-table:gravehollow-cellars.goldRange.max=14`, `elite-affix:rabid.hpMultiplier=4`, `encounter:…`, `item:…`); the report's header names every override |

**The report.** §0 says which floors the content has and which are missing (`runner.expectedFloors`), and which recipes wait for a zone or a class. §1 outcomes per recipe (cleared, retreated, wiped, the chosen hero died, stuck or capped — which must be 0). §2 deaths per delve (reported until James sets targets). §3 the per-floor rows of `07` §8.2 for every floor and party size: rooms, the straight-line walk, fights and elites, ticks to the stairs, the hunter, ambushes both ways, traps, Key and Lock floors, gold found against `07` §2.3's per-floor target, damage taken per hero, downs, deaths, wipes, torches, light at the stairs and how often it was Dark or Pitch, item grades, the curio outcome mix, and the threat band each arrival read. §4 boss floors: kill rate, rounds and the arena mechanics from the delve's `stats.bossArenas` (lanterns out, six candles, tide step 4, the third Throne hunter, braziers, re-calls, returning hunters, parts broken, tells, the lowest light). §4 also measures the back row (the adds behind a barricade or a throne: their share of the enemy side's damage, and how often nobody hits them) and, since Phase 8, **who deals a boss fight's damage** (the boss itself against each add and arena call: the share a boss's own Attack can move); §4b says what brings heroes down (deaths by fight kind, the deadliest fights by floor and monsters, the share of damage from status ticks). §5–§7 fight length, economy and wear (the party's most-worn main hand), exploration, each judged — a boss fight on its rounds to kill (the fights the party won; Phase 8), the rest on every fight. §8 per-class damage, in the delves and from pass A's `tools/balance/class-damage.ts` (the spread is judged). §9 danger you can read: deaths per floor arrival by threat band, and the careful player against the others. §11 the judgement lines: only the `runner.policy` player (textbook) on Normal without Pacts is judged; the careful player, Hard, Dread and Pacts are reported. A balance pass adds a hand-written §13: the rule fixes since the last report, every tuning change with its evidence, what is still off and the smallest fix proposed.

**The scripted players** (`sim/src/policies.ts`). *textbook* explores everything, rests when hurt, takes safe curio choices and descends when the floor is done; *cautious* rests sooner and never gambles; *greedy* opens everything and never retreats; *careful* is the textbook player reading the threat (D-092): it turns back from a floor whose chip reads Deadly (the boss's too) and never enters a room it knows reads Deadly; *random* takes a random legal action. In a fight every hero follows its own AI rule; in a boss fight the hero AI turns on a back-row add it can reach that has out-damaged the boss (round 2). All but random walk the shortest way, clear a revealed trap that shuts the door on it, have the most hurt hero drink their own potion, finish the delve rather than teleport out on its last floor, and never wander: with no legal way on they stop and `runDelveAuto` ends the delve as **stuck** (Phase 7 fixed the Phase 6 soak's r3 ↔ r4 loop on Gravehollow F2, seed 101).

**Debugging one delve.** `node tools/balance/debug-seed.ts --seed 101 --floor 1 --party warden:dwarf,blade:orc --level 4 [--policy careful] [--watch 2:r3]` replays one delve the way the client builds a party and prints its last steps. **A glance at a what-if:** `node tools/balance/summary.ts tools/balance/out/<file>.json …` prints one line per recipe (cleared against its target, boss fights won, deaths per delve). **The big policy sweep:** `GV_SWEEP=1 npx vitest run sim/test/policies.test.ts` (every floor, ten times the seeds; `npm test` runs the small one).
