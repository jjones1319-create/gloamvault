## 6. Reading the report

- A **fight** is 3–6 rounds for ordinary packs (03 §3.6; 06 §8.9 narrows fodder packs to 3–4), elites 6–8, bosses 10–15, hunters 6–8.
- Gold per delve should land near **R = 300** at the Cellars band (03 §8.1), with the full repair bill between 15% and 35% of it (08 §9.3 holds the Warden at ~27%).
- A fully used F weapon should lose 25–35% per delve (03 §6.4; 08 §9.4 says 30%, 3.3 delves to broken).
- The party should be surprised in roughly 10–30% of fights at full light with a Perception-10 party; Dim adds 5 pp, the Cellars family 15 pp (03 §4.2–§4.4).
- Phase 3 caveat: Tiers IV–V, the Crypts and the Vault, Dungeons 2–4 and the Beast Tamer are not in the content yet; a hook the sim has no joint for logs `hookStub` (sim/src/skills/custom/README.md) and §4b lists the ones that fired.

## 7. What changed between the first run on real content and this one

*Hand-written by Claude Code after the run. The "before" is `2026-10-27-content-pass-1-raw.md`: the same content with the Phase 2 numbers, where only the classic four ever cleared (86%), solo Wardens died on floor 2 every time and the three-hero parties wiped at the Captain. Every change below is in `content/` or `tuning.json` (numbers) or is a logged rule reading (`docs/13-DECISIONS.md` D-077–D-086).*

**James's brief of 2026-10-27 set the targets** (D-084): the tower is very difficult and not soloable the first time without levels and gear; soloing is possible with a lot of grinding; party members are the intended way. So the runner now plays every recipe **fresh** (level 1, starter kit) and **ground or levelled** (higher level, better gear), and judges the Cellars clear rate as well as fight length.

Bugs and rule readings found by the runner (fixed in code, logged):
- Burn and Bleed ticked for 0 (no "applying hit" damage reached them) — now 25% / 20% of the hit (D-077).
- A self-cast buff lost a turn the moment it was cast (statuses counted down at the end of the turn they were gained) — a status gained during the holder's own turn keeps its full duration (D-077).
- Every room's wall sconces counted as braziers (+15 light per room: light never fell) — only `shr_campfire` restores light (D-078).
- Part breaks fired on almost every hit on fodder — a typed limb breaks only to its type, an untyped limb only on a critical hit (D-078).
- The Mender never healed herself and wasted heals on pets; a rule whose action could not be done fell straight to a basic attack; "enemy status mark" read only the weakest enemy — the gambit's "ally" is the party's heroes with the actor, an impossible action falls through to the next clause, "enemy status X" means any enemy (D-079).
- A boss's adds fought on after the boss fell; a champion's HP ×2.5 multiplied once per affix — the crew flees when the boss falls; the champion's bonuses apply once (D-081).

Floor 3 (level 4, two champion affixes) and the Captain (HP ×14, Attack ×1.6) are back at 06/07's values: with Final Fantasy levels the party grows through the delve, so the docs' cliff is now the intended difficulty.

Still off target (for Phase 4's and Phase 7's passes, not blockers):
- **The classic four clears 83% fresh** (target 40–75%). The Arcanist (91 damage per action at level 1) and the Mender (70, more than the Blade's 65) out-damage the Ranger (33) by 2–3×: a class pass in Phase 7 (with Tiers IV–V) is the place; under James's unlock model a fresh four-hero party is not a day-one state.
- **Elite fights run 4.8–5.8 rounds** (target 6–8) and **hunter fights 10–16** (n ≤ 5).
- **Weapons wear 9–19% per delve** (target 25–35%) at `08` §9.1's exact k; gold per cleared delve runs 1.4–1.8 R for parties. Both are tuning for Phase 4, when town prices and repairs are on screen.
- Levelled recipes land under the round targets by design (they outlevel the Cellars).
